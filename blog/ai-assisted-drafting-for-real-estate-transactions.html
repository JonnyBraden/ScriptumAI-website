AI-assisted drafting for real estate transactions

17 June 2026 · 3 min read

AI-assisted drafting for real estate transactions applies to one of the most document-intensive areas of transactional practice. A commercial property acquisition, development finance or significant leasing transaction involves multiple agreements, complex interdependencies between documents and a negotiation process that can extend over several months.

The document burden in commercial property

A commercial real estate transaction of any significant size involves documents across several categories: the principal sale and purchase documentation, the financing arrangements, the development or planning agreements if relevant, the lease or tenancy documentation for income-producing assets and a range of ancillary agreements covering access, overage, restrictive covenants and other property-specific matters. Each document type has its own standard structure, its own clause conventions and its own negotiation dynamics.

The volume and complexity mean that document management is a significant component of the total workload on a commercial property transaction. An associate team that is managing several matters simultaneously may find that document production is the binding constraint on how many matters can be handled well at the same time.

Where AI-assisted drafting helps most

In a real estate context, AI-assisted drafting is most valuable at the first draft stage, where producing a well-structured document quickly from firm precedents reduces the time to first exchange and sets the negotiating frame for the counterparty. It is also valuable in the review of the counterparty's draft, where identifying departures from standard positions in a long lease or development agreement requires careful comparison that can be accelerated significantly with the right tool.

Standard clause libraries for common real estate documents, including commercial leases, option agreements and conditional contracts, allow the team to produce consistent, well-structured first drafts without the assembly time that would otherwise be required.

Completion and post-completion documents

The completion pack on a commercial property transaction involves a large number of documents that need to be produced consistently and in the right order. AI-assisted drafting that understands the interdependencies between these documents reduces the risk of inconsistency in the completion set.
