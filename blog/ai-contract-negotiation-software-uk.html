Published 8 November 2026·3 min read
The short answer

For UK law firms, the best AI contract negotiation software keeps a lawyer in control, works inside Word, and learns from what your firm has actually agreed. Scriptum is the strongest fit for mid-sized transactional firms: its negotiation agent answers each round inside limits a partner sets, escalates anything outside them, and builds a firm-owned record of every position taken. Luminance suits in-house teams that want AI to negotiate routine contracts directly with counterparties.

"AI contract negotiation" covers very different products. Some review the other side's markup and suggest redlines. Some answer rounds of negotiation on a lawyer's behalf. One or two will negotiate directly with a counterparty. For a law firm acting under professional duties, the difference matters.

Three levels of AI negotiation

  1. Review and suggest. The AI reads incoming markup and suggests responses. A lawyer does everything else.
  2. Prepare within limits. The AI prepares each round's response inside limits set by a supervising lawyer, and escalates anything outside them. A lawyer approves what goes out.
  3. Negotiate autonomously. The AI negotiates directly with the counterparty within configured rules.

For most law firm work, level 2 is the sweet spot: real capacity gains, with supervision where your professional duties put it.

What to look for in negotiation software

Who sets the limits?

The partner should set how far the AI can move on each point, and the limits should be enforced, not advisory. In Scriptum, anything outside the limits goes straight back to the partner.

Can the client see your limits?

They should not. Scriptum's clients answer questions inside the app without ever seeing your negotiation limits.

Does it learn from your deals?

A playbook says what you would like. A negotiation history says what you actually got. The best tools use both. Scriptum captures every agreed clause with its history, and on Pro adds the firm playbook and counterparty history to the negotiation agent.

Does it track the issues across rounds?

Negotiations sprawl across turns. A running issues list that updates with each round saves the most painful admin in any deal.
