Luminance alternative for mid-sized UK law firms

By Jonny Braden·Published 8 November 2026·3 min read
The short answer

Luminance is a strong platform for organisations handling high volumes of contracts, with modules covering drafting, negotiation, portfolio analysis and compliance, and an option for its AI to negotiate directly with counterparties. Scriptum is the better alternative for a mid-sized UK law firm: it is built for private practice deal teams, keeps a lawyer in control of every response, gives your client and opposing counsel one workspace, and publishes its prices from £79 per user per month.

Luminance is a UK-founded legal AI company and one of the best known in the market. It announced a $75m Series C in 2025 and says it serves more than 700 organisations in over 70 countries, including a quarter of the world's largest law firms.

What is Luminance?

Luminance is a contract AI platform. Bind's 2026 Luminance pricing guide lists its modules as Draft, Negotiate (AI review in Microsoft Word), Analyze (portfolio analysis and obligation management), Comply, Investigate and Collaborate. Luminance's Series C announcement describes Lumi Go, which lets a business send a draft agreement to a counterparty while the AI negotiates on its behalf, and a multi-model approach it calls a "Panel of Judges". Named clients include large companies such as Tesco and AMD as well as law firms. Luminance does not publish its pricing.

Why the difference in control matters for law firms

For an in-house team clearing hundreds of NDAs and supplier contracts, letting AI negotiate routine terms directly with the counterparty can make sense. For a law firm, the position is different. You are acting for a client, under professional duties, and your name is on every turn that goes out.

Scriptum is built on a simple rule: the agent proposes, a named lawyer decides. The negotiation agent answers inside limits your partner has set, and anything outside them goes straight back to the partner. Clients answer questions inside the app without ever seeing those limits. Nothing reaches the other side without approval.

Why Scriptum is the better Luminance alternative for mid-sized firms

  • Built for private practice. Matters, clients, partner sign-off and opposing counsel are part of the design, not configuration.
  • The knowledge belongs to the firm. Agreed clauses and their negotiation history build into a library the firm owns, and the Pro plan adds counterparty history.
  • Priced for firms of 20 to 100 fee earners. Published per-user pricing, billed monthly.

When Luminance may suit you better

  • You are an in-house team managing a large portfolio of contracts and obligations.
  • You want AI to negotiate routine, low-value agreements directly with counterparties.
  • You need compliance monitoring or investigation tools in the same platform.
