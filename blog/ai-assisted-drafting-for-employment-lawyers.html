AI-assisted drafting for employment lawyers

16 June 2026 · 3 min read

AI-assisted drafting for employment lawyers working in a transactional context addresses the document-intensive side of employment practice: service agreements for senior hires, settlement agreements, restrictive covenants, employment warranties and indemnities in corporate transactions, and the TUPE documentation that arises in asset sales and outsourcings.

High volume, consistent structure

Transactional employment work has a characteristic that makes it particularly well-suited to AI assistance: a large number of documents with broadly consistent structures but significant variation in the specific terms. A senior executive service agreement for a new appointment has a clear structure but the specific provisions for salary, bonus mechanics, benefits, notice, restrictive covenants and termination terms will vary significantly by role, seniority and negotiating dynamics. Producing these from a well-maintained precedent base quickly and accurately is exactly the kind of task where AI-assisted drafting delivers clear value.

Settlement agreements are another high-volume document type where consistency and speed matter. The structure is largely fixed by the statutory requirements; the variation is in the specific financial terms, the scope of the claims being settled and the non-disparagement and reference arrangements. Producing a clean settlement agreement quickly from a firm template is a simple task for AI assistance.

Employment aspects of corporate transactions

In corporate transactions, the employment lawyer's role often includes producing employment warranties and indemnity provisions, reviewing the disclosure of employment arrangements in due diligence and advising on the employment consequences of the deal structure. The drafting that arises from this work, including warranty schedules and disclosure letter entries, benefits from the same structured precedent approach as other transactional documents.

TUPE documentation in asset sale or outsourcing transactions involves a specific set of documents that need to be produced consistently: employee liability information, measures letters, election documents and, in complex transactions, collective consultation documentation. Standard document sets for each TUPE scenario reduce the risk of missing a required document under time pressure.

Restrictive covenant consistency

Restrictive covenant provisions are an area where drafting consistency matters particularly: the firm's positions on scope, duration and geography should be consistently expressed across all the agreements where restrictions appear. AI-assisted drafting from a curated position library reduces the risk of inconsistency across the team.
