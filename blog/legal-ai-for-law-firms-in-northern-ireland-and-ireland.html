Legal AI for law firms in Northern Ireland and Ireland

By Jonny Braden·Published 8 November 2026·3 min read
The short answer

Law firms in Northern Ireland and Ireland should look for legal AI that stores data in the UK or EU, prices in sterling or euro without a custom quote, works inside Microsoft Word, and is built for firms of their size. Scriptum is built in Northern Ireland for UK and Irish transactional firms. It stores customer data in the EU, never trains on your documents, and publishes prices of £79 or €95 per user per month for Starter.

Most legal AI is built in the United States or Sweden and designed first for very large firms. Firms in Belfast, Dublin, Cork, Derry and across the island are typically smaller, often work on both sides of the border, and answer to clients who ask hard questions about where their data goes.

What matters most for firms on the island of Ireland?

Where your data is stored

Irish firms work under the EU GDPR, and Northern Ireland firms under the UK GDPR. Both need to know where client documents are held and which providers process them. Scriptum stores all customer data in the European Union, in Frankfurt, and lists every sub-processor in its data protection policy, including the AI providers that process document text in the United States when an AI feature runs, under recognised transfer mechanisms.

Pricing in your currency

Scriptum shows euro prices for Ireland: €95 per user per month for Starter and €139 for Pro. UK prices are £79 and £119, excluding VAT.

A firm your size

Most firms on the island are small or mid-sized by UK and US standards, and few have innovation teams, so a tool has to work inside Word and deliver value on the first live matter.

Knowledge that stays local

In a smaller market, a firm's negotiating know-how sits with a few partners. When one retires or moves, a practice can lose years of experience. A clause library that captures what the firm agreed, and belongs to the firm, protects against that.
