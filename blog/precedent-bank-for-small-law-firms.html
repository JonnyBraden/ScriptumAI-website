How a small firm can build a precedent bank worth having

3 August 2026 · 3 min read · Jonny Braden

A precedent bank worth having is one your lawyers actually use. Most small transactional firms have something they call a precedent bank: a shared drive, a folder of past documents, occasionally a proper document management system. Few of them have a precedent bank that reliably produces better first drafts than starting from a blank page, and the gap between the two is entirely predictable.

What makes a precedent bank genuinely useful

Three things. It has to contain documents that reflect the firm's current positions, not what the firm agreed to under different market conditions several years ago. It has to be findable: lawyers under time pressure will not search a poorly organised drive, and if the search takes longer than starting from scratch, starting from scratch wins. And it has to be trusted: if the team has learned through experience that the precedents on the server have not been reviewed recently, they will draft around them rather than from them.

Building a bank that meets all three criteria without a dedicated knowledge management function is genuinely difficult. The lawyers who know which precedents are good are too busy billing to update the ones that are not. The associates who might help often do not yet know enough to make reliable editorial decisions. The knowledge stays locked in individual files and individual heads, and the precedent bank drifts further from actual practice with every year that passes.

How to build a precedent bank that compounds

The practical answer for a small firm is a system that captures precedent quality as a byproduct of normal work rather than as a separate maintenance project. Each time a lawyer drafts from a precedent, reviews it, marks it up, and works through a negotiation, that process itself is information. Which clauses get changed consistently? Which are kept? Which are being negotiated away in almost every deal because they no longer reflect where the market actually sits?

A platform that captures those signals automatically builds a precedent bank that reflects actual practice rather than aspirational positions. The bank improves with each matter handled, without anyone having to schedule a review session or take time away from billable work to update templates.
