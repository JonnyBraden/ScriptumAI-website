Capacity

How AI drafting tools increase law firm capacity

25 July 2026 · 3 min read · Jonny Braden

Capacity is the quiet constraint on almost every growing law firm. You could take on more matters if you had more hands, but hiring is slow and the pipeline does not wait. So the question becomes a practical one. How do you get more work through the door without stretching the team past breaking point?

Where the hours actually go

In a transactional practice, a surprising share of a lawyer's day goes on the mechanical parts of drafting. Finding the right precedent. Rebuilding a schedule the firm has produced a hundred times before. Carrying defined terms across a suite of documents and checking they still line up. None of it is the clever work clients pay a premium for, yet it eats the time that could go to another matter.

What changes with AI-assisted drafting

A good drafting tool takes the first pass at that mechanical layer. It produces an initial draft from the firm's own precedents, keeps defined terms consistent, and flags where one clause depends on another. The lawyer moves straight to judgement, which is where their value sits. The effect is not that a single document takes dramatically less time. It is that the same team can carry more matters at once without the wheels coming off.

Capacity, not speed, is the prize

This is an important distinction, especially for a firm that bills by the hour. The goal is not to record fewer hours on a deal. It is to lift the ceiling on how much work the firm can hold at any one time. A team that was running flat out on eight matters can take a ninth and a tenth, because the routine drafting no longer soaks up every spare moment.

For a firm on hourly billing, that framing matters more than any efficiency pitch. Extra capacity means saying yes to more of the work that comes your way instead of turning it down or pushing timelines out. It means a busy month does not automatically become a late one. And it means the growth in your pipeline is not capped by how quickly you can recruit.
