Data security

Your data never mingles

Every matter is fully isolated within its own secure environment, accessible only to the individuals you invite and subject to your internal approval process. Your organisation's documents, client information and deal data are never shared across firms or matters. For law firms with strict confidentiality obligations, Scriptum is built with that responsibility at its core, so you can adopt AI with confidence, not compromise.

  • Firm-level isolation enforced at the database layer
  • Visibility controls separating internal notes from shared content
  • The knowledge layer is built from your firm's work alone, and belongs to your firm alone
