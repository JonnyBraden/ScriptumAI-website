    That is Scriptum

    Every chapter drew on the same thing: your firm's own record of what it has negotiated and agreed. Scriptum builds that record from the deals you already do, keeps it inside your firm, and puts it to work in Word, in the Clause Library, in the agents and in the conversation with your client.

    1. 1
      In WordThe wording your firm agreed before, one search away, with the outcome and source attached.
    2. 2
      Clause LibraryA firm-owned record that grows with every deal, filed for you.
    3. 3
      AgentsDrafting, review and negotiation that propose. Your lawyers decide, within limits your partners set.
    4. 4
      Matter chatClient conversations in one place, screened so firm-only material never reaches the client.
