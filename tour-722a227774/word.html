SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT

relating to Ember Grid Limited · Draft 1 · Carrick & Shaw LLP for the Company

7. Pre-emption on transfer

7.1

Save as permitted by clause 9 (Permitted transfers), no Shareholder shall transfer any Share except in accordance with this clause 7.

7.2

A Shareholder wishing to transfer Shares shall first give a Transfer Notice to the Company specifying the number of Shares and the price per Share.

8. Drag along

8.1

[Drag-along threshold: insert from precedent]

8.2

A Drag Notice may only be served where the Proposed Buyer has made a bona fide offer on arm's length terms.

8.3

Each Called Shareholder shall receive the same consideration per Share as the Drag Majority, applied in the order of priority set out in Article 4 (Return of capital).

8.4

The Drag Majority shall give a Drag Notice to the Company not less than 10 Business Days before the proposed date of transfer.

9. Permitted transfers

9.1

A Shareholder may transfer Shares to a Privileged Relation or to the trustees of a Family Trust, subject to clause 9.3.

9.2

An Investor may transfer Shares to a member of its Fund Group without restriction.
