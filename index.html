How it works

Tracked changes and email chains? You and your clients have suffered for long enough.

For over 30 years, lawyers have collaborated the same way: emailing Word documents back and forth, hunting through inboxes for the latest version, and hoping nobody missed a footnote. And when a lawyer leaves, everything they learned about drafting, negotiating and counterparties leaves with them. Scriptum AI brings the workflow into the AI age, saving your firm time, reducing cost, giving clients an experience that matches the quality of your advice, and keeping the knowledge inside the firm where it belongs.

01

Create a matter

Open a new matter for any transaction, whether an investment round, acquisition or commercial contract, and upload draft documents or templates in one place.

02

Invite your team

Add individuals from your own organisation, your client and opposing counsel with granular role-based permissions. Each party only sees what they need to, when they need to.

03

Draft with AI

Use AI to generate first drafts, suggest amendments, ask your client questions or insert comments for discussion.

04

Review and approve internal changes

Did you ask a junior lawyer to handle the initial draft? Review and approve their amendments, or add your own. Approve or reject amendments line by line.

05

Collaborate with your client

Share amended documents directly with your client through the platform, collect their feedback and let Scriptum prepare draft amendments for your approval right away. Prefer email? Export a table of changes and collaborate the old-fashioned way. The choice is yours.

06

Negotiate

Received the next turn of the draft? Upload it for an instant redline, with each amendment extracted and awaiting your input, plus an AI-generated summary and issues list so you can focus on the negotiation, not the admin.

The knowledge layer

Your most valuable asset shouldn't walk out the door with your lawyers.

Every firm's real edge is what its people know: how you draft, what you concede, what counterparties accept, why a clause reads the way it does. Today that knowledge lives in individual heads, inboxes and old markups. Scriptum captures it as a byproduct of lawyers simply doing their work, and builds it into a firm-specific, practice-area specific knowledge asset that powers AI agents trained on your playbook, not a generic model's.

Captured, not curated

Clause choices, amendments, review notes and client answers are captured automatically as your team works. No knowledge management chores, no extra fields to fill in.

Firm-specific agents

Your knowledge layer powers drafting and review agents tuned to your firm and each practice area, so suggestions reflect your precedents and positions, not the internet's.

Owned permanently

The knowledge asset belongs to your firm. When a lawyer moves on, their drafting patterns, negotiated positions and counterparty experience stay behind and keep working.

Isolated by design

Your data never mingles. Every matter sits in its own secure environment, never shared across firms or matters, so you can adopt AI with confidence, not compromise.

Who it's for

Built for the deals that matter

Scriptum AI is built for lawyers whose work is primarily document-driven: drafting, negotiating and closing. If your practice involves any of the following, Scriptum was built for you.

M&A and Private Equity / Venture Capital

Share purchase agreements, investment agreements, subscription documents, shareholders' agreements, disclosure letters. M&A and VC transactions involve multiple parties, tight timelines and documents that go through dozens of turns. Scriptum keeps every party aligned, every amendment tracked and every approval documented from term sheet to close.

Commercial Contracts

Supply agreements, service agreements, NDAs, licensing deals. Commercial contracts are negotiated constantly, often in high volume. Scriptum's AI-assisted drafting, intelligent questioning workflow and opposing counsel redline tools mean your team spends less time on admin and more time on the substance of the deal.

Commercial Property

Employment

Settlement agreements, service agreements, restrictive covenant agreements, consultancy contracts. Scriptum's approval workflows, client collaboration tools and AI-assisted drafting mean you can handle higher volumes without compromising on quality or confidentiality.
