1. Who we are

Scriptum AI Limited ("Scriptum", "we", "us") is a company registered in Northern Ireland with company number NI740697. Our registered office is at 4 Ballybarnes Meadow, Killarn, Northern Ireland, BT23 4GZ. We run tryscriptum.ai, the Scriptum matter workspace and the Scriptum add-in for Microsoft Word (together, the "Service").

Questions about this policy or your data go to info@scriptumai.co.

2. The two roles we play

We are the controller of data we decide to collect for our own purposes: website visits, demo and contact requests, sales contacts, account administration, billing and security.

We are a processor of the documents and other content that a law firm or legal team puts into the Service. That customer decides what goes in and why, and we handle it on its instructions under our Data Protection Policy.

If your name or details appear in a document held on a matter, for example as a shareholder, director or signatory, the law firm or legal team running that matter is the controller. Please contact it first. If you do not know who that is, write to us and we will help where we can.

5. How we use AI

Parts of the Service send matter content to AI providers so that it can be parsed, classified, searched, summarised, drafted and reviewed. At the date of this policy those providers are OpenAI and Anthropic.

No training. We do not use customer documents to train AI models, and we use these providers under commercial API terms that do not permit them to train their models on that content. Those terms may allow a provider to hold inputs for a limited period to detect misuse.

A lawyer decides. AI output is a proposal held for a named lawyer to accept, change or reject. The Service does not make decisions about individuals that produce legal or similarly significant effects, so it does not carry out solely automated decision-making within Article 22 of the UK GDPR.

6. Who receives your data

  • Advisers and authorities. Our professional advisers, and regulators, courts or law enforcement where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect legal rights.
  • A buyer or investor, under a duty of confidence, if we sell or reorganise our business or raise funding.

7. Marketing

We may email people at law firms and legal teams about Scriptum where their role makes it relevant. We use professional contact details only, which we collect from the person or from public sources such as firm websites. Every marketing email tells you how to opt out. Reply or write to us at any time and we will stop, keeping only enough detail to make sure we do not contact you again.

8. Where your data is stored, and transfers outside the UK

All account data and matter content is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), which the UK treats as providing adequate protection. We do not keep it in a database or document store anywhere else. Some providers listed in section 6 process data in the United States. That includes the AI providers, which receive the relevant text from matter content when an AI feature runs and return the result. For those transfers we rely on the UK-US data bridge where the provider is certified under it, or otherwise on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguard that applies.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each firm's data is separated from every other firm's at the database. Access is limited by role and our own access is limited to what is needed to run and support the Service. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private and tell us straight away if you suspect misuse. Our Data Protection Policy sets out our security measures in more detail.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data deleted;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting what was done before.

Write to us to use any of these. We reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs longer. Where we hold the data as a processor, we will pass your request to the customer concerned.

13. Children

The Service is for legal professionals and the people they work with on business matters. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data as users. If you think we have, please tell us and we will delete it. A minor's details may still appear in a document that a customer uploads, in which case that customer is the controller.

14. Complaints

Please tell us first if you are unhappy with how we have handled your data. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay. You can also complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office, at any time at ico.org.uk.
