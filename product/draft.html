Intelligent questioning flow

Client answers that become amendments

Tired of using footnotes to ask your client questions about a draft? Insert a question on a specific clause, receive your client's response, and Scriptum automatically prepares a suggested amendment based on that response, ready for your approval within the draft document.

  • Questions attach to specific clauses, not buried in email threads
  • Client responses feed directly into proposed drafting
  • Nothing enters the document without a lawyer's approval

  • Clear reporting lines so nothing leaves the firm unapproved

The knowledge loop

Every draft teaches the system how your firm drafts

When a lawyer edits an AI suggestion, corrects a summary or approves a clause, that decision is captured into your firm's knowledge layer. Over time, drafting suggestions stop looking like a generic model's output and start looking like your firm's precedents, in your house style, for your practice areas.

  • Clause choices and corrections captured as a byproduct of normal work
  • Firm-specific and practice-area specific drafting agents, tuned to your playbook
  • Knowledge stays with the firm when people move on
