Every clause your firm has agreed, kept

Most firms have a precedent bank that nobody maintains and a handful of partners who remember what was actually agreed. Scriptum builds the clause library automatically from the documents your lawyers already draft and negotiate: the wording, who it was agreed with, which side you acted for, and every round it took to get there. Automatic capture

Built from the work, not from a KM project

Nobody has to file anything. As drafts, turns and signed versions move through a matter, Scriptum parses each document, maps the clauses to your firm's taxonomy and records the context: matter type, client, document type, counterparty and the side you acted for. Lawyers confirm the handful of uncertain matches and move on.

  • Clause mapping across five practice areas: corporate, commercial, employment, real estate and banking
  • Provenance on every clause so you always know where wording came from
  • Confirmations improve matching for the next document

Owned by the firm

An asset that stays when people move on

The library is your firm's data, built only from your firm's matters, and never used to train models or shared with anyone else. When a senior lawyer leaves, what they knew about a counterparty's habits or where a cap tends to land does not leave with them. It is already in the library, attached to the clauses and the matters where it was learned.

  • Firm-level isolation enforced at the database layer
  • No-training commitment flowing down to every model provider
  • Export at any time; the data is yours
